Reverse CAD 1.0 — first iPhone trial

Capture a stationary object with a LiDAR-capable iPhone, inspect saved RGB/depth/pose evidence, annotate contacts and damage, confirm critical dimensions, and generate an editable bridge handle, cup saddle handle, or flat/L bracket.

The companion service must run on your Mac. Use the same reachable local network. In Service settings, enter http://48.local:8767 and the private pairing token from var/service/pairing-token on the Mac. Allow Camera and Local Network access. Keep the Mac awake. Start the service with ./scripts/start_service.sh --lan from the Reverse CAD project.

Begin with the included synthetic scan replay, then follow PHONE_TEST_PLAN.md for a real calibration object, repeated scans, independent measurements, fit coupons and a replacement fit test. Export the raw scan package to preserve the first trial as regression evidence.

Manual and deterministic intent workflows are available. Live AI requires a separately configured server API key and was not exercised in automated validation. Unsupported devices offer explicitly labeled replay/import and limited camera/manual capture.

Automated tests do not establish real iPhone measurement accuracy, printed fit or strength. Check all critical dimensions and retain the Not tested fit state until you physically verify the print.
